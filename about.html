About Us

The story behind your favorite cup

Our Story

Cape Bean Coffee Co was born in 2020 in the heart of Sea Point. What started as a small cart with one espresso machine has grown into Sea Point's favorite community coffee spot.

We believe coffee is more than a drink. It's a reason to slow down, connect, and create. Every bean is locally roasted and every cup is made with love.

Our Values

☕ Community First: We host open mic nights, art shows, and free wifi for students. This is your space.

🌱 Quality Beans: We source directly from ethical farms in the Western Cape and roast in-house weekly.

♻️ Sustainability: Compostable cups, reusable discounts, and zero food waste. Good for you, good for the planet.
