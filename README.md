## Project Description
Cape Coffee Bean is a local artisan coffee shop based in Cape Town. The website is designed to showcase our specialty coffee blends, fresh pastries, and cozy cafe atmosphere. It aims to connect coffee lovers with our story, menu, and location.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- To create an online presence for Cape Coffee Bean to attract new customers
- To display the menu, opening hours, and location of the shop
- To allow customers to send enquiries and contact the business
- To share the brand story and commitment to locally sourced beans

## Sitemap
- Home - Welcome, hero image, and featured products
- About - Story of Cape Coffee Bean and our values
- Products / Services - Coffee menu and pastries
- Enquiry - Form for custom orders and questions
- Contact - Address, map, hours, and contact details

## Technologies Used
- HTML5 for structure
